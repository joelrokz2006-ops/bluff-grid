THE HOUSE RULES

BLUFF GRID · BACK ROOM · EST. 1931
  1. Stake your credits and flip tiles on a 5×5 grid. Every safe tile lifts your multiplier — true combinatorial odds with a slim 3% house cut. Hit a mine and the stake is gone.
  2. Cash out any time after your first safe tile. Reveal every safe tile and the grid pays out automatically.
  3. Sal vouches for tiles while you play. He is not bound by honesty — the riskier the grid, the more he lies (30% · 45% · 60%). Flip a vouched tile to trust him, or press CALL HIS BLUFF if you smell a lie.
  4. The tell: when he's lying, his eyes dart and a sweat drop falls. Usually. Keep your eyes on him while he speaks — his ledger tracks how often he's been straight with you.
  5. Catch him lying and the mine is defused — ×1.6 to your multiplier. Call it wrong and your multiplier is halved and the tile burns.
